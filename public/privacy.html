PrivaPDF Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

PDF files

PDF files are processed in your browser. PrivaPDF does not upload, store, or inspect the contents of your documents on a server.

Analytics

Optional Firebase Analytics is disabled by default. It is enabled only when you select “Allow analytics.” If enabled, PrivaPDF collects general usage events such as page visits and successful exports. It does not send PDF contents, filenames, text, redaction coordinates, or document metadata.

You can decline analytics at any time by clearing the site’s local storage or browser data.

Local storage

PrivaPDF stores your analytics consent choice locally in your browser. Uploaded files and editing state are not sent to PrivaPDF servers.
